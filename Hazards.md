## Bodybog
When walking over a bodybog make an Intellect roll to notice the ground is soft. Running on it will reveal that the ground is untrustworthy, but it is too late at that point: make an Agility roll to retreat to safety or tear through the bog. Size 1 creatures jumping on it will also break through. Bodybogs get their name from the collection of bodies slowly mummifying in the bog. Tales exist of mummies rising from the bog, but are rarely believed. 
> “I asked a Librarian once about bog mummies. She said she had only heard stories—nothing confirmed. She also said it is reasonable to assume that some kind of plant-based life or insect could use the mummy as a puppet or vehicle of sorts. Best to stay away from the bogs.”
> - Winston Finds, retired Seeker
