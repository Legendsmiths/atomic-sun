# Perks
## Ace
You may treat a natural 1 rolled on a d20 as a natural 20 and a natural 1 rolled on any boon as a natural 6. 
## Lucky
If you have the Lucky perk, you may treat a natural 7 on a d20 as a natural 20. Once per Watch you can make a Luck roll to succeed on any roll you just made. 
## Skilled
Specify a tool, weapon, or specific type of action with which you gain **1 boon**. 
## Expertise
All boons with a value of 6 result in a give you a Stunt Point. 
## Mastery
Ignore **1 bane** or sacrifice a boon to gain a Stunt on success. 
## Driver
You can make a Luck roll to succeed on any vehicle related roll. Once you use this perk you lose access to it (Luck ends). 
## Exoframe
You can make a Luck roll to succeed on any exoframe related roll. Once you use this perk you lose access to it (Luck ends).
