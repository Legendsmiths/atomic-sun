# Dice Rules
## Critical Failure
A modified result of 0 or less may trigger a Critical Failure result if one exists, otherwise it is a simple failure. 
## Failure
A modified result less than the target number required to succeed. 
## Complication
A natural 1 or 13 results in a complication. 
## Ace
You may treat a natural 1 rolled on a d20 as a natural 20 and a natural 1 rolled on any boon as a natural 6. 
## Lucky
If you have the Lucky perk, a natural 7 is a natural 20. 
## Unlucky
If you have the Unlucky affliction, a natural 13 results in a failure as well as a complication. 
## Success
A modified result greater than or equal to the target number required to succeed. 
## Stunt
A natural 20 grants you a stunt to add to the success of your action. 
## Critical Success
A modified result of 20+ and at least 5 greater than the target number required to succeed. This may trigger a Critical Success result if one exists, otherwise it is a simple success.
