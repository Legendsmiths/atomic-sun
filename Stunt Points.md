# Stunt Points
## 1 Stunt Point
* Gain **1 boon** for a single roll until the end of your next turn or grant **1 boon** to an ally for a single roll until the end of their next turn. 
* Gain +1 damage per 1d6 of damage rolled. 
* Perform a stunt as part of or immediately following the action that generated the Stunt Point. 
* Gain an extra Reaction that must be be used before the end of your next Turn. 

## 2 Stunt Points
* Move up to your Speed before the end of your turn in addition to your normal movement. 
* Gain +2 damage per 1d6 damage rolled. 

## 3 Stunt Points
* Take another action before the end of your turn. 
* Gain +3 damage per 1d6 damage rolled. 

## 4 Stunt Points
* Take another turn before the end of the round.
* Gain +4 damage per 1d6 damage rolled
