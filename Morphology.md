# Morphology
## Humanoid
Mutated humans. 
## Animoid
Mutated animals that have evolved human-level sentience. 
## Human
Genetically stable humans with no mutations descended from pre-Blast humanity. Humans do not mutate as a result of radiation, but they can succumb to mutation through xenoartifacts, genetic manipulation, or chimera infection.

## GMPC Morphology
### Fungoids
Humanoid and animoid zombies infected by Sweetblight fungus. 
### Glitchbots
Glitchbots have damaged core processing units and corrupted programming. They pose a threat to anything that does not fit into their current operational models. The term was adopted to distinguish them from robots which are generally accepted throughout society. 
### Podlings
Human-looking plants that behave like humans but are nothing more than lures for Greenhearts. 
### Xenomorphs
Aliens and their alien pets. 
### Chimeras
Humanoid, animoid, or insectoid mutants whose mutations are out of control and infectious. 
### Insectoids
Insect intelligence differs significantly enough from animal intelligence. Attempts to communicate with insectoids have always failed. At best, the insectoid may ignore a human, but whenever presented with even a small group the interaction hostile. Insectoids do not intend to share the world with any other life forms. 
### Flora
Mutated plants abound. They are the primary reason most adventurers stick to the roads. Plants do show signs of intelligence, but their intelligence is as indecipherable as that of insects. Some plants are mobile, but hunter-predators are uniquely rare to the point of myth. Still, many plants have evolved to trap or ensnare creatures for other predators and scavengers. The waste from this relationship nourishes the plants.
